Free AI video and image prompt tools, open source

UniqAI is an app for AI photos, video and chat. These are the free tools we built around it: they write, check and collect the prompts that AI video and image models respond to. Each one runs in your browser, and the code is on GitHub.

Try in your browser

Five tools, nothing to install. Open one, paste or pick an idea, copy the result into the model you use.

01 / Storyboard builder

Video Director

Turn a one-line idea into a timed storyboard and a paste-ready AI video prompt.

What you get. A beat-by-beat shot list and one prompt per model, shaped for Kling 3.0, Veo 3, Wan 2.5, Seedance or Runway, with each model's length and audio limits applied.

Try it in your browserGitHub repoMake a video in UniqAI

02 / Prompt gallery

AI Video Prompt Gallery

Search real AI video prompts, filter by model and copy the one you need.

What you get. Each prompt comes with its model, mode, duration, aspect ratio and a short note on why the structure works. Free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.

Try it in your browserGitHub repoOpen UniqAI video

03 / Ad script checker

UGC Ad Director

Check a UGC ad script before you spend money generating it.

What you get. A lint for the hook, the AI label, claims and fake testimonials, then a first-frame image prompt plus Kling and Veo 3 prompts for every shot.

Try it in your browserGitHub repoAnimate an image in UniqAI

04 / Image prompt checker

no-plastic

Stop AI portraits from looking plastic by fixing the prompt first.

What you get. A 0 to 100 score, the exact words that cause waxy skin and a fill-in photo brief to copy. It checks wording only and cannot see your image.

Try it in your browserGitHub repoMake an image in UniqAI

05 / Russian text checker

deslop-ru

Find the phrases that make a Russian draft sound machine-written.

What you get. Highlights stock AI phrasing in Russian text with the reason and a plainer fix for each hit. It runs in your browser and finds patterns; it does not prove who wrote the text.

All repositories

Every repo is one focused thing and uses only the Python or Node standard library.

  • video-directorShot-by-shot AI video prompts in each model's own syntax.
  • awesome-ai-video-promptsCurated prompt list with model, mode, duration and aspect. CC BY 4.0.
  • ugc-ad-directorUGC ad scripts with a lint that refuses fake reviews and unlabelled AI presenters.
  • ai-video-pricingAI video cost per second, with source URL and check date on every row.
  • no-plasticRewrites an image prompt into a photographic brief and lints it.
  • suno-producerSuno style prompts, meta-tags and lyric structure, in English and Russian.
  • model-routerPicks the cheapest LLM that still passes the task, with dated prices.
  • token-dietRewrites a prompt into compact English and counts tokens before and after.
  • skill-shrinkerCompresses SKILL.md and AGENTS.md files and checks every MUST and NEVER survived.
  • deslop-ruFinds and rewrites the phrases that make a Russian draft sound generated.

Sister project

Music instead of video? HablaMusic builds free Suno prompts and lyrics for reggaeton, cumbia and funk carioca, and has its own GitHub organisation.

A note on models: UniqAI runs Nano Banana for images and Kling and Veo for video. Where a tool also writes prompts for other models, those notes are written for those models, which UniqAI does not run. Model names are trademarks of their owners.
